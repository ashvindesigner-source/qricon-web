Frequently asked questions

Answers about scoring, teams, tournaments, and more. Still stuck? Email support@qricon.in.

Getting started

How do I create a team?

Open My teams from the bottom tab, tap Create team, add your team name and logo, then add players by phone, contacts, or QR scan.

How do I start a match?

From the drawer, tap Start a Match. Choose teams, set overs and venue, complete the toss, pick your openers and bowler, then begin scoring.

Where can I see my matches?

Open My Cricket from the drawer or bottom tab. You will find live, upcoming, and completed matches along with your tournaments.

Live scoring

How do I record runs and wickets?

On the scoring screen, tap run buttons for each ball. For wickets, use the Out button and choose the dismissal type, then select the next batter if needed.

How do I change the scorer?

Open scoring shortcuts and choose Change Scorer. Pick a QricOn player — they get a notification to accept scoring, then you confirm. After that the match appears in their My Cricket so they can score. You can also share a scoring QR code.

Can I undo the last ball?

Yes. Use Undo from the scoring shortcuts menu to remove the most recent scoring action when you entered something by mistake.

Tournaments & teams

How do I create a tournament?

From the drawer, tap Add Tournament. Set dates, location, and format, then add teams and schedule matches from the tournament detail screen.

How do I invite players with a QR code?

Open your team profile and use the QR option. Other players can scan it to view the team or join from their QricOn app.

Can I follow other teams?

Yes. Open a public team profile and tap Follow to keep track of teams you are interested in.

Players & profile

How do I find other cricketers?

Use Find cricketers from your profile or the home suggestions list. Search by name, city, or mobile number to discover registered QricOn players.

Where are my career stats?

Open My Cricket Career from the drawer to view batting, bowling, and match history based on games scored on QricOn.

How do I update my profile?

Open My Profile from the drawer. You can update your photo, location, playing role, and other details from there.

Grounds & venues

How do I find grounds near me?

Open Grounds from the drawer to browse registered venues, see match history at each ground, and filter by your location.

Can I register a new ground?

Yes. From the Grounds screen, tap register and add the ground name, location on the map, and optional photos.

Go Ad Free

What is Go Ad Free?

Go Ad Free removes ads in the app for ₹199 for 2 months. You can buy it from the Go Ad Free button in the app header or from Preferences.

How do I contact support?

Email us at support@qricon.in, or use Feedback inside the app.
