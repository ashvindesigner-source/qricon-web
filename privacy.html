Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Introduction

QricOn ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the QricOn mobile app, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, mobile number (for SMS OTP login), profile photo, location, date of birth, playing role, and cricket preferences. We also collect match, team, tournament, scoring, and ground data you create or participate in within the app. Device and usage information such as app version and platform may be collected to improve reliability and support. If you purchase Go Ad Free, we store purchase status, product id, amount, and entitlement end date; payment card details are handled by the app store, not by us.

How we use your information

We use your information to provide core app features such as live scoring, team and tournament management, player profiles, search, rankings, and nearby matches or grounds. We also use it to personalize your experience, maintain account security, respond to support requests, and improve app performance.

How we share information

Your public profile information, team participation, match statistics, and other cricket activity may be visible to other QricOn users according to how you use the app. We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers who help us operate the app, or when required by law.

Location information

If you allow location access, we may use your location to suggest nearby matches, tournaments, grounds, and players. You can control location permissions in your device settings at any time.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request account-related changes or deletion by contacting us.

Your choices

You can update your profile details in the app, manage notification preferences, and control device permissions such as location, contacts, and camera. You may also contact us with questions about your data or to request corrections.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

QricOn is not intended for children under 13 without appropriate parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information without consent, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date on this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at support@qricon.in.
